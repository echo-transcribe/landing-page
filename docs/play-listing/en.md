# Google Play listing: English (en-US)

## Title

Echo: Offline Transcription

## Short description

Private voice notes: transcripts, summaries and tasks, made on your phone.

## Full description

Say it once. Echo writes it down.

Echo turns meetings, lectures, interviews and voice notes into text, summaries and tasks, right on your Android phone. Nothing is uploaded: after a one-time model download, everything works without internet, even in airplane mode.

ACCURATE, ON YOUR PHONE
• Whisper Large V3 Turbo on phones with 8 GB of RAM, a smaller Whisper model on others
• Your phone's own offline recognizer, where available, for live text and instant transcripts
• Around 100 languages, detected automatically
• Add names and special terms to the dictionary, and Whisper spells them your way

KNOWS WHO SAID WHAT
• Speakers are labelled automatically
• Name someone once and the name carries through the note and your exports
• Tap any line to hear that moment of the recording

SUMMARIES AND TASKS
• A short summary follows right after the transcript
• Echo finds what needs doing and turns it into tasks
• Say "tomorrow at 10" and it reminds you then
• Today's tasks on the home screen, the rest in the calendar
• Every task links to the line it came from

ORGANISED
• Browse notes and tasks by day in the calendar
• Sort notes into folders like Work, Personal or Ideas
• Search every transcript, title and summary
• Your edits never overwrite the original recording

IMPORT AND SHARE
• Import MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, OGG and Opus files, and the audio of MP4 and WebM videos
• Share as plain text for free
• Export Markdown, SRT subtitles and the original audio with Pro

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Audio, transcripts, summaries and tasks are stored only on your phone
• No account and no sign-up
• We don't run servers for your notes, so we can't leak, sell or train on them
• Back up your notes to a file you keep, and restore them on a new phone

FREE AND PRO
Echo is free to download. The free version transcribes the first 2 minutes of every note, with a summary and tasks, and you can make as many notes as you like. Your whole recording is always saved.
Pro removes the time limit, makes summaries and tasks from the whole recording, and adds Markdown, SRT and audio export. Choose monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase. Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in Google Play.

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 10 or newer on a 64-bit phone
• Summaries and tasks need about 4 GB of RAM and a one-time download of about 1 GB
• Live text needs Android 13 or newer and your language installed on the phone

Summaries and tasks come from a small AI model on your phone, so they can be wrong. Check important ones; every task can be edited.
