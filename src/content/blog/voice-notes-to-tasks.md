Talking is faster than typing. On the way home you can list everything you need to do tomorrow in thirty seconds. The hard part is turning that into a list you will actually look at, with reminders at the right times.

## Why not just set reminders by voice?

Voice assistants are good at one reminder at a time: "Remind me to call the dentist at 3." But a real list comes out mixed up. You talk about what happened in the meeting, then mention two things you promised, then a deadline on Friday. A voice note catches all of it; what is missing is someone who pulls out the tasks.

## How Echo does it

1. **Record or import.** Tap the mic and talk, or import a recording of a meeting.
2. **Echo checks for plans.** A small AI model on your phone first asks whether you talk about things you or others will do. Explanations and how-tos are usually left out.
3. **Tasks with your times.** "Tomorrow at 10" or "on Friday" is read from your words and checked by fixed rules, so the time does not depend on the AI guessing.
4. **Reminders.** Echo reminds you at the time you mentioned. Today's tasks are on the home screen and the rest in the calendar.

If you said a day but no time, the reminder comes at 9:00 that day. If you said no time at all, it comes an hour after the task was made. You can change the time in any task.

Every task links to the line it came from, so you can check what was actually said with one tap.

## Tips for better tasks

- **Say who and when.** "I'll send Maja the offer by Thursday" gives a clearer task than "the offer".
- **One task per sentence.** Long chains of "and then… and also…" are harder to split.
- **Check after important meetings.** The model is small enough to run on a phone, so it can miss a task or make an odd one. Edit, add or delete tasks as needed.
- **Allow exact alarms.** For reminders on the minute, let Echo set exact alarms in Settings, Task reminders. Otherwise Android may deliver them a little later.

## What it needs

Tasks need a phone with about 4 GB of RAM and a one-time download of about 1 GB. Times are read in every language Echo transcribes. Notes in other languages get tasks written in English with the quote in your language, unless you download the optional multilingual model (2.6 GB), which writes them in the language of the note.

In the free version, tasks come from the first 2 minutes of each note. Pro reads the whole recording.

Nothing is sent anywhere: tasks and reminders stay in Echo on your phone. More on the [home page](/#tasks).
